# /clinicas

Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: the clinic owner arriving from an ad, on a phone, comparing Horaya with WhatsApp and a shared calendar. Job: decide to open the panel. Action: `Comenzar gratis` to `https://app.horaya.mx/signup`. Proof: the real price, the three published trust lines, and the product frame. No invented metrics or quotes.

Constraints: incumbent Horaya visual system. Homepage chrome stays off this route. No “sin tarjeta”, no setup-time claim, no refund that is not already true. Intake forms disclosed as Equipos y Empresas before the panel mock. Empresas stays a quiet mailto.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A single sales argument in the manual’s order, said in Horaya’s clinic nouns. Refuses the brochure sequence that tells the visit three times before the price.

OWN-WORLD: The site already shipping: Outfit, paper `#fafaf8`, ink `#171717`, brand blue only on the primary action, soft shadow, product frames as the picture. No second palette.

STORY: The owner sees the result, the price, the pain closed by that result, what happens after the click, the honest gap where proof will go, the comparison with the chat, the data promises, the objections, and the same button again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Copy first. H1 “Tu clínica, fuera del chat y del calendario compartido.” One `Comenzar gratis`. Under it, the free plan and the exact Equipos launch price, per seat, both periods. The panel frame sits beside that on wide screens and below the button on a phone, with the paid-form line above the frame.

FORM: The manual’s ten-block order, brief-pinned by the approved campaign decision. No concept-seed. Social proof is omitted until a clinic signs it. Position: the locked sales sequence, not a new visual world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
